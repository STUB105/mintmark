One Million Gallons Privacy Policy

Last updated October 29, 2026

One Million Gallons does not ask for your name, email or an account, and it has no ads. Your progress is saved on your computer, and Steam may back it up with Steam Cloud. We do not collect any personal information.

Who we are

One Million Gallons ("the game") is made by Mint Mark and sold on Steam. This policy explains what information the game uses and why.

Information the game keeps

Your money, upgrades, unlocks, skins, stickers and settings are saved in a file on your computer. We do not send this progress to our own servers, and we cannot see it.

Steam

The game is bought, downloaded and run through Steam, which is operated by Valve Corporation. If Steam Cloud is turned on, Steam copies your save file to your Steam account so it carries over between computers. Achievements you earn are recorded on your Steam account. Valve handles this information under the Steam Privacy Policy. We never receive your payment details.

No ads, no tracking

The game contains no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking.

Children

One Million Gallons is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.

Support and contact

For help with One Million Gallons or questions about this policy, email Mint Mark at mmintmmarkstudios@gmail.com. We usually reply within a few days.
